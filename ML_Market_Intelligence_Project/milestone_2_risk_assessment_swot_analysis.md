## Part 1: Risk Assessment

### What is Risk?
Risk is the possibility that something may negatively affect a project.
* **Examples:** Lack of customers, insufficient money, strong competitors, technology problems, operational problems.
* **Key Question:** *What can go wrong?*

### Why Do We Assess Risk?
* Identify possible problems before implementation
* Understand the seriousness of each risk
* Support better project decisions
* Reduce unexpected problems

### Five Types of Project Risk

1. **Market Risk**
   * **Meaning:** Uncertainty about customer demand and market acceptance.
   * **Example:** Customers may not be interested in the product.
   * **Key Question:** *Will customers actually want our product?*

2. **Financial Risk**
   * **Meaning:** Possibility of money-related problems.
   * **Example:** Project requires ₹10 lakhs but only ₹5 lakhs is available.
   * **Key Question:** *Do we have enough financial resources?*

3. **Competition Risk**
   * **Meaning:** Risk created by existing or new competitors.
   * **Example:** Several strong companies already offer similar products.
   * **Key Question:** *Can our project compete successfully?*

4. **Technical Risk**
   * **Meaning:** Risk related to technology, development, or technical skills.
   * **Example:** Project requires advanced AI but the team lacks expertise.
   * **Key Question:** *Can we technically build the project?*

5. **Operational Risk**
   * **Meaning:** Problems that may occur while running the project.
   * **Examples:** Staff shortage, poor management, resource or maintenance problems.
   * **Key Question:** *Can we operate the project successfully?*

## Part 2: Risk Scoring

Risk is measured using a simple 1–5 scale:
* **1** = Very Low Risk
* **2** = Low Risk
* **3** = Medium Risk
* **4** = High Risk
* **5** = Very High Risk

### Example: Overall Risk Score
| Risk Category | Rating |
| :--- | :--- |
| Market Risk | 3 |
| Financial Risk | 4 |
| Competition Risk | 4 |
| Technical Risk | 2 |
| Operational Risk | 3 |

$$\text{Average Risk} = \frac{3 + 4 + 4 + 2 + 3}{5} = 3.2 \quad (\text{Medium Risk})$$

### Why Calculate Overall Risk?
* Five separate risk values can be difficult to interpret.
* One overall score gives a quick view of project risk.
* **Higher score = Higher risk.**
* The score supports project decision-making.

## Part 3: SWOT Analysis

### What is SWOT Analysis?
**SWOT** = **S**trengths, **W**eaknesses, **O**pportunities, **T**hreats.
It provides a structured view of the project's current position.
* **Internal:** Strengths and Weaknesses
* **External:** Opportunities and Threats

### SWOT Matrix Breakdown

| Perspective | Positive | Negative |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Internal** | **Strengths**<br>Advantages, unique technology, skilled team, low cost, strong product quality.<br>*Question: What are we good at?* | **Weaknesses**<br>Limitations, limited budget, small team, lack of experience, high development cost.<br>*Question: What do we need to improve?* |
| **External** | **Opportunities**<br>Favorable situations, growing market, increasing demand, new customer segments, new technology.<br>*Question: Where can we grow?* | **Threats**<br>External hazards, strong competitors, market changes, regulations, rapid tech changes.<br>*Question: What can harm our project?* |

## Part 4: Feasibility Assessment

### What is Feasibility Assessment?
Feasibility means determining whether a project is **practical, achievable, and worth implementing**.
* **Core Question:** *Can we realistically implement this project?*

### Factors in Feasibility
* **Market:** Is there enough demand?
* **Financial:** Do we have sufficient resources?
* **Technical:** Can we build the required technology?
* **Risk:** Are the risks manageable?
* **Competition:** Can we compete in the market?

### Feasibility Scoring Scale

| Score Range | Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **80 – 100%** | **Highly Feasible** |
| **60 – 79%** | **Feasible** |
| **40 – 59%** | **Moderately Feasible** |
| **0 – 39%** | **Not Feasible** |

## Case Study / Example Project

**Project Name:** AI-Based Online Learning Platform

* **Risks:** Medium market risk, high financial risk, high competition risk, low technical risk, medium operational risk.
* **Strengths:** AI personalization, easy accessibility.
* **Weaknesses:** Limited budget, small team.
* **Opportunities:** Growing online education market.
* **Threats:** Established competitors, rapid technology changes.

### Final Evaluation Result
* **Feasibility Score:** $72\%$
* **Status:** **FEASIBLE**
* **Interpretation:** The project has risks and competition, but the market opportunity and technical possibility make it feasible with proper planning.
